# External sources for essay/aside topic ideas

Sites to survey for essay and aside topic ideas on bradfordfults.com, alongside the site's own writing history (`app/content/posts/`).

## Canonical list

| Source | URL | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aeon | https://aeon.co | Philosophical/humanist long-form essays; science-meets-culture; personal-transformation narratives. Matches the `personal` essay/aside lane (society, recreation, books). |
| The Pragmatic Engineer | https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com | Engineering leadership, career trajectories, AI's effect on engineering practice. Matches the `business` essay lane (leadership, management, software). |
| Talking Points Memo | https://talkingpointsmemo.com | Current US political/authoritarian-drift reporting. Matches the `personal/society` essay lane, especially the throughline started by "Paroxysm, Paralysis." |
| The Paris Review Daily | https://www.theparisreview.org/blog/ | Literary craft/voice — models sentence-level writing rather than argues ideas. Feeds the book-review asides and reflective personal essays. |
| Letras Libres | https://letraslibres.com/ | Spanish-language Latin American criticism and creation (descended from Octavio Paz's *Vuelta*). Native-language upstream for the bilingual posts and the Borges/Poniatowska lineage. |
| Rest of World | https://restofworld.org/ | Ground-level, non-US tech reporting. Bridges the business and personal/society lanes without repeating Pluralistic's polemic or TPM's US focus. |
| Pluralistic (Cory Doctorow) | https://pluralistic.net/ | Daily structural critique of the software industry itself — monopoly, enshittification, platform power. Highest-yield recurring browse for contrarian hooks in the leadership lane. |
| Wellcome Collection Stories | https://wellcomecollection.org/stories | Essays on bodies, health, medicine, and physical experience. Matches the embodiment register of the nudism-as-freedom essay; nothing else in the list touches the body. |
